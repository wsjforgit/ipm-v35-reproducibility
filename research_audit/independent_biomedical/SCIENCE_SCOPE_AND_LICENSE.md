# Experimental scope, inferential limits and upstream rights

1. **Evaluation units and gold:** SciFact's evidence abstracts and NFCorpus's health-document relevance labels originate from their benchmark authors, not from the researcher's 40 BCKB silver qrels. This is independent *task reference construction*, not a breast-cancer multisource clinician gold study. Neither benchmark supplies all four source roles, treatment contraindication judgments or patient safety labels.
2. **Interaction vs retrieval gain:** The nDCG@10 factorial interaction is the difference-of-differences, NOT a performance improvement over raw flat BM25. Always report the four condition means and G11−G00 alongside the interaction. In NFCorpus G01 degrades dramatically while G11 barely exceeds G00.
3. **Query dependence:** Document relevance lists overlap across some test queries (SciFact 65/44850 pairs, NFCorpus 4262/52003 pairs). Even external author-judged qrels need not satisfy topic-exchangeability for confidence interval claims; no p-values are attached here.
4. **No-hit and evaluator protocol:** Fifteen NFCorpus queries have zero hits in G00 and G10, but not in G01 and G11. Empty retrieval is scored zero; there are no fake documents. NIST official program checked only truly ranked queries and final means were adjusted to include zero-query cases.
5. **Pre-registration:** The experiment's 2x2 representation/query choices, test split, tokenizer, 48-token budget, stoplist, metrics and reporting were frozen in protocol before reading BEIR qrels. Code changes later were documented input-handling fixes (accept real no-hit rankings, score empty as zero); no effectiveness-based parameter tuning or post-hoc benchmark selection.
6. **Four-source silver-reference sensitivity:** Relevance definitions themselves were changed by source-family deletion/selection. Results are only descriptive robustness to label composition, not a randomized or externally validated mechanism effect.
7. **Licensing:** BEIR is a benchmark wrapper, not sole licensor of third-party datasets. SciFact original repository claims/annotation metadata cite CC BY 4.0 and abstracts ODC-By 1.0; another downstream card uses CC BY-NC. The safest course adopted here is to publish only authored programs and author-derived numerical aggregate outputs, without copying third-party questions, scientific abstracts, original judgments or paper texts. NFCorpus upstream permissions likewise control reuse. Links: https://github.com/allenai/scifact/blob/master/LICENSE.md and https://github.com/beir-cellar/beir/wiki/Datasets-available .
8. **Publisher policy:** The repository is public. A final CMPB submission must check the author's institution-approved SCIE JCR Q1 year/category and applicable single-/double-anonymous code policies; no journal submission is made by this software freeze.
